Information handled

The website is a product-discovery preview and does not provide visitor accounts, checkout or payment processing. The cart shortlist and wishlist store product names in your browser's local storage; these lists are not sent to AKINI.

The Contact page embeds a HubSpot form. If you submit it, the form may collect your first name, last name, email address, optional phone number and message so AKINI can respond. The form is provided through HubSpot (EU1 form embed). The information is transmitted to HubSpot and made available to the AKINI account managing the form. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

Before commercial launch, AKINI must confirm and publish the applicable legal basis, retention period, HubSpot contractual role and data-processing terms, any international transfers, and how to exercise privacy rights in relation to form submissions. Contact mansurahmed833@gmail.com for questions or to request access or deletion.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, browser details and server logs to provide and secure hosting under its own policies. Images are loaded from Unsplash, which may receive technical request data.

Cookies and local storage

The cart uses browser local storage. AKINI does not currently intentionally use analytics or advertising cookies. Third-party services may use their own technologies.

Your rights
