About AKINI

I buy. I choose. I love.

AKINI is a product-discovery website focused on Pets, Home and Lifestyle. Our aim is to help visitors explore useful products and discover options from independent retailers.

How AKINI works

AKINI is an affiliate-style discovery website. We do not manufacture, stock, sell, pack or ship products, and we do not process payments or orders. When a visitor chooses a retailer link, the purchase takes place directly on that retailer's website and is subject to the retailer's terms.

Our categories

Pets: ideas and products for pets and their everyday needs.

Home: home, kitchen and living inspiration.

Lifestyle: everyday accessories and lifestyle finds.

Transparency

Some links may be affiliate links. If a visitor makes a qualifying purchase through such a link, AKINI may earn a commission at no extra cost to the visitor. Product prices and availability are set by retailers and may change.

AKINI is currently being developed. Product examples, prices, ratings and retailer links on the preview website may be placeholders and should not be treated as verified offers.
