A Comfortable Home Office Setup for Small Spaces

A productive home workspace does not require a separate room. A few thoughtful choices can make a desk area more comfortable, organised and easier to put away at the end of the day.

Choose the right working surface

Pick a stable surface with enough depth for your screen, keyboard and the way you work. Keep frequently used items within easy reach and leave space for writing or documents. If you use a dining table, a small organiser can help you clear work materials when the table is needed for meals.

Set screen and chair positions

Place the screen so you can look ahead without repeatedly bending your neck. Keep shoulders relaxed and feet supported; use a footrest if needed. Adjust the chair and desk to your body rather than trying to fit your body to the furniture. Take regular movement breaks and change position during the day.

Manage light, cables and sound

Where possible, place the desk near natural light without strong screen glare. Use task lighting for darker hours. Route cables so they do not cross walkways, and avoid overloading power strips. Headphones may help with focus, but keep volume at a comfortable level and remain aware of your surroundings.

Make it easy to close the workday

Use a tray, drawer or laptop stand to store work items when you finish. A simple end-of-day reset helps separate work from home life, especially in shared or small spaces.

AKINI takeaway

Prioritise a stable surface, a posture that feels comfortable, good lighting and a quick way to pack everything away.
