Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In the current AKINI development version, the cart shortlist and wishlist are saved in browser local storage. Local storage is not technically the same as a cookie.

HubSpot contact form

The Contact page does not load the HubSpot form automatically. It loads the form only after you select the “Load contact form” button. After activation, HubSpot (EU1) may use cookies or similar technologies depending on the account settings and form configuration. The exact cookies, purposes and durations must be checked in the live browser and HubSpot account before launch. Loading the form is not a substitute for any consent that may be legally required.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this site and external image services may receive technical request information. Retailers or affiliate networks may use cookies or similar technologies after you follow their links; their own policies apply.

Analytics and advertising

AKINI does not currently intentionally deploy analytics or advertising cookies. If these or affiliate tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before activation.

Managing browser data

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing local storage may remove your cart shortlist.
