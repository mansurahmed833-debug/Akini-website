Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

In the current AKINI development version, the cart shortlist and wishlist are saved in browser local storage. Local storage is not technically the same as a cookie.

Contact form

The third-party HubSpot contact form is currently disabled. The Contact page provides an email link only. If a third-party form is reintroduced, its cookies and similar technologies, purposes and durations must be checked and this policy updated before activation. Any non-essential tracking technologies must be handled in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

Third-party services

GitHub Pages hosts this site and external image services may receive technical request information. Retailers or affiliate networks may use cookies or similar technologies after you follow their links; their own policies apply.

Analytics and advertising

AKINI does not currently intentionally deploy analytics or advertising cookies. If these or affiliate tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before activation.

Managing browser data

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Clearing local storage may remove your cart shortlist and wishlist.
