AKINI Editorial Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

AKINI is an independent, personal product-discovery project covering Pets, Home and Lifestyle. Our purpose is to help readers understand what to check before choosing everyday products. AKINI is not a retailer, manufacturer, veterinary service or product-testing laboratory.

How guides are prepared

Guides are written to explain practical selection factors such as measurements, materials, compatibility, maintenance, safety and intended use. We aim to use clear language, distinguish general guidance from product-specific claims and update pages when important information changes.

Product information and testing

Unless a page explicitly says that a product was independently tested, readers should not assume AKINI has bought, handled or tested it. We do not invent customer reviews, ratings, awards, prices or performance results. Any sample product cards marked “Preview only” are illustrative examples, not current offers or verified recommendations.

How products may be selected

When real products are introduced, we intend to compare relevant features against the needs described in each guide. Availability, specifications and prices can change; readers should confirm current details with the retailer before purchasing. We will not describe a product as the best choice without explaining the criteria behind that judgement.

Affiliate links and independence

AKINI currently has no active affiliate links and receives no commissions. If an affiliate relationship is introduced, it will be disclosed clearly near relevant links and on the affiliate disclosure page. Commercial relationships will not be presented as independent testing or as an endorsement by a retailer or manufacturer.

Corrections and contact

If you spot an error or believe a guide needs clarification, contact mansurahmed833@gmail.com. Please include the page URL and the detail you would like us to review.
